An interactive three-dimensional cassette machine. Audio stays on your device. Use E to open the door, Space to play or pause, S to stop, M to mute, X to disassemble. Tap arrows for previous or next; hold arrows to scan. Up and down adjust volume. Keys 1–4 focus the left, center, right, and top; Escape returns to the overview. Drag archive tapes toward the door and release to load. Click an edition on the table advertisement to change the machine; click the paper corner to pull the next sheet. Top selectors support click and drag. Click a person to greet them. Hold Shift while dragging knobs for fine adjustment.

This machine needs WebGL 2. Please enable browser hardware acceleration and reload.
